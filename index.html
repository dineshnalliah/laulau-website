our story

growing up in a biracial chinese american home in the midwest, folding wontons every holiday while drinking wisconsin old fashioneds, i’ve always had to find a balance between worlds.

Lau Lau celebrates the fusion of Asian and American cultures, and the idea that belonging is something we keep shaping together. That shows up in our specialty drinks, our pastries, and the cultural events we host.

We choose small, value-driven local vendors and approach every decision with a people-first mindset. For us, Lau Lau is more than coffee. It’s a step toward what the world could and should be.
