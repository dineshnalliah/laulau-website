our story

Named after my family, Lau Lau is deeply personal and grounded heavily in my own Chinese American experience. Growing up in a biracial Chinese-American home in the midwest, folding wontons every holiday while drinking Wisconsin Old Fashioneds, I’ve always had to find a balance between worlds and identities. Understanding that this feeling of “not enough one or the other” is a common sentiment, I’ve always dreamed of creating a space where people felt safe and seen in all their life experiences.

At Lau Lau, we proudly celebrate the fusion of Asian and American cultures while also acknowledging that the notions of culture and belonging are intricate and multidimensional. We embrace the fact that these concepts are constantly evolving, and we aim to be a space where we can reclaim and honor the richness of mixed heritage. For us, this means incorporating elements from my own Chinese heritage as well as the broader Asian diaspora, and infusing them into specialty drinks, pastries offerings, and cultural events.
